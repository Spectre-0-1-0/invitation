# The Digital Scrapbook Blueprint: Structure & Flow

This document defines the physical and narrative structure of the College Memory Archive. It serves as the master blueprint for how the story of the class of 2025 is told.

---

## PART 1: THE BOOK ITSELF

### Book Format
The scrapbook is a **Landscape Narrative**. It is not a vertical feed; it is a horizontal journey across time and emotion. While technically implemented as a website, the mental model is a thick, well-loved album with physical depth and a distinct beginning, middle, and end.

### Narrative Flow
1.  **The Arrival (Beginning):** The user "opens the cover." This is the moment of invitation—the transition from the present into the world of memory.
2.  **The Journey (Middle):** A thematic exploration of life. It moves from the chaos of early years to the bonds of friendship and the achievements of the final days.
3.  **The Farewell (Ending):** The emotional peak. A space for final words, quiet reflection, and the symbolic "closing of the book."

---

## PART 2: CHAPTER DESIGN

### Chapter 0: The Cover
*   **Emotional Purpose:** Anticipation and Identity.
*   **Story Purpose:** Establish "Who" this book belongs to (The Class of 2025).
*   **Visual Identity:** Heavy texture, a central "main" photo, and handwritten names.
*   **Transition:** A "lifting" or "turning" motion into the first page.

### Chapter 1: How It Started
*   **Emotional Purpose:** Nostalgia for the "New."
*   **Story Purpose:** Documenting the first days, move-in, and the awkward early energy.
*   **Visual Identity:** Bright, messy, lots of "first" photos (student IDs, dorm rooms).
*   **Transition:** A chronological shift toward the people who made it home.

### Chapter 2: The People
*   **Emotional Purpose:** Belonging and Love.
*   **Story Purpose:** A deep dive into the faces that define the era.
*   **Visual Identity:** Polaroid-heavy, clusters of friends, handwritten superlatives.
*   **Transition:** Moving from individuals to shared experiences.

### Chapter 3: Chaos & Memes
*   **Emotional Purpose:** Joy and Inside Humor.
*   **Story Purpose:** The "private" world of the students—the jokes only they understand.
*   **Visuals:** Scattered notes, "bad" photos, screenshots, and doodles in the margins.
*   **Transition:** A shift from the lighthearted to the monumental.

### Chapter 4: Events & Adventures
*   **Emotional Purpose:** Excitement and Scale.
*   **Story Purpose:** The big moments—festivals, trips, sports, and all-nighters.
*   **Visuals:** Ticket stubs, map fragments, panoramic "spreads."
*   **Transition:** From the "Doing" to the "Succeeding."

### Chapter 5: Achievements
*   **Emotional Purpose:** Pride and Validation.
*   **Story Purpose:** Celebrating the hard work and final milestones.
*   **Visuals:** Diploma fragments, stage photos, elegant serif typography.
*   **Transition:** The realization that the journey is over.

### Chapter 6: Messages
*   **Emotional Purpose:** Connection and Gratitude.
*   **Story Purpose:** Final farewells and shared promises.
*   **Visuals:** A wall of varied notes, different handwriting, overlapping tape.
*   **Transition:** A final, quiet page.

### Chapter 7: The Last Page
*   **Emotional Purpose:** Emotional Closure.
*   **Story Purpose:** The "Thank You" and the sign-off.
*   **Visual Identity:** Minimalist, a single powerful photo, "The End."

## PART 3: PAGE TYPES

| Page Type | Purpose | Structure | Emotional Role |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Cover Page** | Entry | One central image + title | Invitation |
| **Journal Entry** | Context | Dominant text + small photo | Narrative / History |
| **Memory Cluster** | Personal | 3-5 overlapping photos + tape | Intimacy |
| **Photo Spread** | Impact | One full-page image (bleed) | Awe / Peak Moment |
| **Group Photo** | Connection | Central grid + names | Belonging |
| **Quote Page** | Emphasis | Large handwritten text | Reflection |
| **Timeline Page** | Rhythm | Linear path of small notes | Orientation |
| **Message Wall** | Community | Dense, messy collage of notes | Collective Love |
| **Chaos Page** | Humor | Random doodles and screenshots | Joy / Inside Joke |
| **Reflection Page** | Depth | Ample whitespace + poignant text | Bittersweet Nostalgia |
| **Closing Page** | Exit | Symbolic image + "The End" | Finality / Legacy |

## PART 4: PACING

The scrapbook follows a **Rhythmic Wave** rather than a steady climb.

*   **The Intro (Chapters 0-1):** Low intensity, high curiosity.
*   **The Peak (Chapters 2-4):** High energy, fast-paced, dense pages, laughter-driven.
*   **The Descent (Chapters 5-6):** Slower rhythm, more whitespace, focus on individual words.
*   **The Echo (Chapter 7):** Single-note intensity. Quiet and heavy.

**Rhythm Rule:** Never have three "heavy" (text-dense) pages in a row. Break reflection with chaos. Break chaos with a single, clear photo.

---

## PART 5: MEMORY PLACEMENT PHILOSOPHY

1.  **Hierarchy of Importance:** Major memories (graduations, big wins) get a "Photo Spread." Minor memories (coffee shop study sessions) get "Clustered" or "Tucked."
2.  **The "Placed" Rule:** No element is perfectly centered. Everything must have a 1-3 degree rotation to feel placed by a human hand.
3.  **The "Crowded" Rule:** Pages in the "Middle" (Chapters 2-4) should feel slightly overstuffed—like there was too much life to fit into one book.
4.  **The "Quiet" Rule:** The first and last chapters must have generous margins. Emotion needs space to breathe.

---

## PART 6: DISCOVERY DESIGN (PLACEMENT STRATEGY)

Surprises are not accidental; they are strategically placed to reward "dwelling."

*   **The "Behind the Photo" Note:** Place handwritten metadata *under* an overlapping photo corner.
*   **The Margin Doodle:** Place humorous sketches in the extreme whitespace of "Achievement" pages to ground the pride in reality.
*   **The Folded Corner:** Use "dog-eared" corners on pages that have "Hidden Chapters" (long stories).
*   **The Tucked Ticket:** Place ephemeral items (rendered tickets, maps) so they appear to be "poking out" from between pages.

---

## PART 7: BOOK COMPLETION

### The Emotional Ending
When the user reaches Chapter 7, the experience must shift.
*   **The Feeling:** A mix of "I'm glad I was there" and "I'm sad it's over."
*   **The Action:** Before leaving, the user should encounter a space that feels like a "Signature" page—where the weight of the collective class is felt.
*   **The Sign-off:** The final image should not be a celebration, but a quiet moment (an empty campus path, a sunset over the library) to signify that the class has moved on, leaving this book behind.

---

**Architect's Note:** This structure is the skeleton. The content is the soul. The design must never let the skeleton show through.
