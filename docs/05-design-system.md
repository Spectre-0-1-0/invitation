# 05 Design System

## Emotional Core: "Elegant Nostalgia"
The design should feel like a bridge between the classic university legacy (timber, stone, parchment) and a modern digital archive.

## Color Palette
- **Primary (Heritage Navy):** `#1A2B48` - Stability, tradition, depth.
- **Secondary (Champagne Gold):** `#D4AF37` - Celebration, achievement, warmth.
- **Accent (Burnt Sienna):** `#A0522D` - Nostalgia, earthiness, autumn vibes.
- **Background (Soft Parchment):** `#FDFCF8` - Paper-like feel, easy on eyes.
- **Text (Charcoal):** `#333333` - High readability.

## Typography
- **Heading (Serif):** *Playfair Display* or *Lora*. Used for titles and quotes to evoke "Yearbook" elegance.
- **Body (Sans-Serif):** *Inter* or *Public Sans*. Used for descriptions and interface elements for modern clarity.
- **Secondary Display (Mono):** *JetBrains Mono* (light weight). Used for dates and small metadata to give a "catalogued" feel.

## Spacing & Layout
- **Grid:** 12-column on desktop, 4-column on mobile.
- **Margins:** Generous whitespace to allow content to breathe and feel "curated."
- **Border Radius:** Soft corners (`8px`) rather than sharp or fully round.

## Motion Principles (Framer Motion)
- **Entrance:** Staggered fade-ins for list items (profiles, gallery cards).
- **Transitions:** Smooth "page slide" or "shared layout transitions" when moving between list and detail views.
- **Micro-interactions:** Subtle scale-up on hover/tap for interactive elements.
- **Emotional Peak:** Parallax scrolling on the Home hero and Tribute page.

## Core Components
1. **MemoryCard:** Image/Video with overlay title and date.
2. **SeniorProfileHeader:** Elegant layout with headshot and large quote.
3. **TimelineNode:** Vertical line with dots connecting chronological events.
4. **MessagePostIt:** Digital representation of a physical note.
5. **GlobalNav:** Minimalist navigation that stays out of the way.
