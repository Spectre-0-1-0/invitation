# 01 Project Overview & Vision

## Vision
The **College Memory Archive** is a digital sanctuary for graduating seniors to revisit and celebrate their collective journey. It serves as a living legacy, capturing the ephemeral moments of college life—from high-stakes achievements to late-night inside jokes—in an elegant, nostalgic, and easily shareable web experience.

## Goals
1.  **Nostalgia-Driven Storytelling:** Create an emotional experience that resonates with graduating students.
2.  **Collective Archive:** Serve as a central hub for photos, videos, and messages that might otherwise be scattered across social media or private chats.
3.  **Low Friction Access:** A public, mobile-first site that requires no login to view, making it easy to share on graduation day.
4.  **Long-Term Preservation:** A static-first architecture ensuring the site remains accessible and performant for years to come.

## Target Audience
-   **Graduating Seniors:** The primary users who will browse their own history and that of their peers.
-   **Undergraduates (Juniors):** Looking for inspiration and paying respects to the outgoing class.
-   **Faculty & Staff:** To appreciate the growth and impact of the graduating cohort.
-   **Family & Friends:** To share in the celebration of their loved one's achievements and memories.

## Emotional Tone
-   **Nostalgic:** Warm, slightly grainy or sepia-toned influences mixed with modern clarity.
-   **Personal:** Feels like a hand-crafted scrapbook rather than a corporate portal.
-   **Elegant:** Sophisticated typography and smooth transitions (Framer Motion).
-   **Joyful:** Celebrating victories and shared laughter.

## Success Criteria
-   Visitors spend time exploring multiple pages (Profiles, Gallery, Timeline).
-   High sharing rate on social platforms (WhatsApp, Instagram stories).
-   Seniors express that the site accurately "captured the vibe" of their college years.

## Project Boundaries (v1)
- **No Authentication:** All content is public.
- **No Database:** Data is managed via local JSON and MDX files.
- **No Real-time:** No live chat or notifications.
- **Static First:** Optimized for performance and longevity.
