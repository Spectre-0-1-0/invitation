# 02 User Journey

## Core Flow: "The Nostalgic Discovery"
This journey focuses on a senior discovering the site, exploring memories, and feeling a sense of completion.

1.  **Entry (Landing Page):**
    - Visitor arrives via a shared link (e.g., Instagram bio or WhatsApp group).
    - Encounter a high-impact, emotional "Hero" section with a poignant headline and a montage of college life.
    - Call to action: "Enter the Archive."

2.  **Exploration (Gallery & Timeline):**
    - Visitor browses the **Memory Gallery**, filtering by "Candid," "Events," or "Milestones."
    - They switch to the **Timeline**, scrolling through the chronological history from Freshman orientation to Senior week.
    - Each item is clickable to reveal more context (captions, related people).

3.  **Connection (Senior Profiles):**
    - Visitor searches for themselves or friends in the **Senior Profiles** directory.
    - They view individual profile pages containing:
        - Major and graduation quote.
        - Personal "Hall of Fame" (achievements).
        - Shared memories they appear in.

4.  **Reflection (Message Wall & Memes):**
    - Visitor reads through the **Message Wall**—a collage of farewell notes and "thank yous."
    - They visit the **Hall of Memes** for a dose of shared humor and inside jokes unique to their cohort.

5.  **Closure (Final Tribute):**
    - Visitor reaches the **Final Tribute** page—a curated video or scrollable experience designed to be the "final goodbye" and emotional peak.

6.  **Sharing:**
    - Visitor uses the "Share Archive" button to send the site to their family or post it on social media.

## Secondary Journey: "The Proud Parent/Faculty"
- Enters through a specific senior's profile link.
- Views achievements and messages dedicated to that senior.
- Explores the broader gallery to see the context of the senior's college life.
